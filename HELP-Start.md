# The Start tab: Help

This page is the help for the **Start** tab. It is also the fold called
**About this page** at the foot of that tab, built from this same file, so
the two always say the same thing. The help for the rest of the app is
`HELP.md`, which is the **Help** tab.

## What this page is

A place to do one small good thing, and then, if you like, another one a
little bigger.

It opens with three choices. Pick any one. There is no wrong one, and none
of them takes more than a moment. When you have done it, the page says what
just happened, a plank is laid on the bridge at the top, and you are offered
the next one.

That is all it does. It is here so that the first thing you meet in this
app is something you can do, and feel, in under a minute.

## How it goes

1. **Pick one of three.** The first three are thoughts: someone you're glad
   to know, something near you that you like, something that went right.
   You have done it by the time you have read it.
2. **For someone else, or for you?** After the first, the page asks who the
   next one is for. Both are good answers. You can change your mind at any
   time with the line under the choices.
3. **A little bigger each time.** The next choices ask slightly more: a
   word said aloud, a glass of water, a one-line message, a real thank-you.
   Past the first, these are things you go and do. The page waits.
4. **Say how it went.** There are three answers, and all three are fine.
5. **Five planks is a bridge.** When the fifth is laid you are across. You
   can build another, or stop.

The three answers:

- **I did it** lays a plank and offers one a little bigger.
- **Not just now. Keep it for later** keeps that one for you and offers
  others about the same size.
- **Something smaller** offers smaller ones.

**That's enough for now**, under the choices, stops at any point. What you
built stays on the page, and **Carry on** picks it up where you left it.

## The bridge

The picture at the top is two banks with the outline of a bridge between
them. The outline is there from the start: the way across can be seen
before any of it is built. Each small good you do lays one plank, and the
dot, which is you, walks onto it.

## What it keeps, and what it does not

- **What you've crossed**, under the choices, lists the things you did and
  the ones you kept for later. It is your own list, shown only to you.
- It lives on this page only. It is not saved, it is not sent anywhere, and
  it is gone when you close or reload the page. That is so whether you are
  a guest or signed in.
- Nothing you do here becomes part of a stream or a map, and none of it
  reaches the AI.

## What it will never do

- **It does not grade you.** There are no points, no marks, no streaks and
  nothing to lose. Stopping, choosing something smaller, and keeping
  something for later are all ordinary things to do.
- **It does not push.** Every next step is offered, never required, and it
  is one step bigger, not ten.
- **It does not decide what is good for you.** It offers small goods of two
  kinds and you pick. Which way you go is yours.

The page does keep one thing to itself: a rough sense of how big a step you
have been taking, read from what you chose and how it went. It uses that
only to size the next three choices. It is not a mark, it is never shown,
and it is gone with the page.

## Why it is here

The app is for the question "what is worth doing?" A question like that can
feel like a chasm: the far side is plain enough, and the way across is not.
What usually makes a gap impassable is not its width. It is that no bridge
can be seen.

So the first thing the app does is let you cross a very small gap and
notice that it felt good. A crossing that feels good is one you will want
to make again, and a slightly wider one next time. The rest of the app, the
maps of a person's world and the words on their links, is for the wider
gaps: seeing where you are, where you want to be, and what would get you
there.

## What is not built yet

This is a first version, and it is small on purpose.

- The choices are a fixed list of thirty, written by hand. They do not yet
  come from your own world or your own words.
- Nothing carries over to another visit.
- It is not joined to the rest of the app. A small good you do here does
  not appear on a map or as a move toward a goal.
- It reads how big a step to offer from two things only: what you picked,
  and what you said afterwards.
