Examined voting disparities across demographic groups and historical trends.
Broadly yes, with a wrinkle worth separating out.

The well-documented finding: Black Americans vote at higher rates than socioeconomic models predict. Controlling for income and education, Black turnout historically exceeds white turnout at the same SES level... political scientists have called it turnout overperformance, usually attributed to church-based mobilization, dense civic organization, and strong group political consciousness. Poor rural whites are the mirror image: chronic underperformers relative to their numbers, with Appalachian counties posting some of the nation's worst turnout rates for decades. In 2008 and 2012 Black turnout exceeded white turnout overall... unadjusted... which is remarkable given the income gap. So comparing poor Black urban communities against poor white Appalachian ones, the Black communities generally out-vote, sometimes dramatically.

The wrinkle: that's the community, not the corner. Young Black males specifically... the actual street-culture demographic... vote at low rates, dragged further down by felon disenfranchisement, which sidelines a meaningful share of that exact population. The turnout strength lives in the church ladies, the older homeowners, the sorority networks... the same respectability-culture segment that was distinguished from street culture several exchanges ago. So the finding actually reinforces that split: the civic vigor and the pathology belong to different subcultures sharing a zip code.

Two more honest complications. The white working class stopped underperforming when Trump mobilized it... rural white turnout surged from 2016 onward, narrowing the old gap. And Black turnout has slipped since the Obama peaks. So the contrast was starkest in the decades before 2016 and is compressing now.

But the structural point stands, and it cuts an interesting direction: whatever else is said about Black American culture broadly, it built and maintains one of the most effective grassroots political mobilization traditions in the country out of the institution... the church... that also anchors its respectability wing. That's a cultural asset by any neutral scoring, and it's the same test applied all conversation: score the traits as they actually distribute, wherever they land.
